I tried to get a visa from some other African countries 'as well, but every country's embassy was refusing to recognize this World Service Authority passport. Not knowing what to do, I thought, "BABA is asking every day if I've left India. So let me just go to Thailand and try to get visas from there."

"I believe in a Universal philosophy and a Universal ideology. For me the whole world is one. Hence I keep this World Service Authority passport. I am a World Citizen."

My statement impressed him. He went inside and stamped the visa and returned my WSA passport with a Thai visa that was good for thirty days. The next day the Central Office Secretary Dada, hearing how I was successful in my bid for a Thai visa, also went to the same embassy to try and get a visa there too. Unfortunately for him, that day the regular staff was there on duty – they refused to give him a visa.

Knowing how often BABA was asking if I had left India or not, that same night I got my visa I crossed into Nepal and took a flight from Kathmandu to Bangkok. There I went all around the city making rounds in the different embassies - Middle Eastern and African - but none were willing to accept my WSA passport. I was stuck in Thailand.
